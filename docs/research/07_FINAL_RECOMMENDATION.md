# 07 · Final recommendation

1. **Best solution:** A small Godot 4 GDScript Cinematic Action Director that layers branching, QTE state and recovery over AnimationPlayer timelines, the existing humanoid player/tree, native Camera3D and the game's present Dialogue Manager.
2. **Most valuable repository:** Telltale Explorer contributes the strongest choreography evidence. TelltaleToolKit's independent chore and Dlg type model is the best cross-check. Godot Event Sequencer contributes a practical awaited-event pattern.
3. **Reuse or build:** Build the thin director and input gate. Reuse native tools and the deployed Dialogue Manager. The sequencing and QTE addons were useful test references. Their lifecycle, timing and controller gaps would require adaptation comparable to a purpose-built gate.
4. **Existing rigs:** Yes for the standalone kit at the playback seam. Two original kit rigs loaded with 65 bones each and executed jab, roll and stagger on Godot 4.6.3. Actual The Static integration and animation contact depend on checking its deployed rig and control ownership.
5. **Smallest proof:** Two existing actors, an AnimationPlayer method marker, one 3D cut, one mapped timed action and two animated reactions, followed by distinct resume lines. This research package includes that runnable exercise and assertion logs.
6. **Next assignment:** Add one typed director beat in a disposable The Static checkout, connect its real actor/dialogue/camera/save adapters and demonstrate both routes on a physical controller. Then stage the stool choice and bartender acknowledgement.

The reusable unit is a beat with a clear entry pose, timed audiovisual tracks, a player challenge and a readable exit. A small number of such beats can express a fight, a chase or a struggle. Keep timing-critical animation and prop reactions together, place a compact prompt during readable anticipation, and give a missed dodge its own follow-through and story response.

**Evidence limit.** The Telltale data audit finds evidence for choreography assets, scene/agent/resource bindings, timed placement and separate dialog/script constructs. It does not identify which retail asset or evaluator handled an individual QTE.
