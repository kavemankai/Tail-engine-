# Humanoid prototype integration

The Episode 0 cast now instantiates the imported PlayerCharacter, Rook and Juno through a TEActor adapter. The outer body remains under Flow and the exploration controller; the child kit physics is disabled. Movement velocity drives the kit's locomotion and CAD clip calls dispatch to provisional in-place animations. The Episode 0 cast is also used by many QTE lab examples. The target/cinematic fixture cast remains unchanged so its special sockets are preserved.

The clip mappings are stand-ins: guard, hits, dodges and grapples are not matched paired contact choreography. They need authored clips, retargeting and contact review. Audio is unvoiced. Physical controller, production quality and all route-specific visual results have not been accepted.

The user's Halogen bar has been staged into the Episode 0 bar location, offset to roughly align with existing Tail Engine marks. The Tail Engine-specific copy removes all 16 decorative bar-stool instances; the stage's single animated story stool uses the supplied stool mesh. This is an early fit: the bartender faces away in the captured QTE shot. Gameplay routes, camera placement, lighting, collisions, and interaction markers need dedicated retesting and adjustment. The scene was authored for Forward+; the current Compatibility renderer reports unsupported SSR and volumetric fog. A short automated non-recording run before the stool removal at 1280x720 on an RTX 3050 Laptop GPU, with VSync disabled, sampled 281 frames across startup, movement, QTE and scene transitions: mean 36.51 ms/frame and 95th percentile 33.70 ms. This is a rough single-run measurement, not a stable benchmark or a performance acceptance result.

Open with Godot 4.6.3 and matching export templates. `content/humanoid/demo_capture.tscn` is an automated capture: it moves the player in the bar, then enters the directional counter lab and deliberately does not respond to the prompt. `adapter_probe.gd` checks QTE dispatch to each rig. From the project root, run `python tools/verify.py --godot <path-to-godot>` for the regression suite.
